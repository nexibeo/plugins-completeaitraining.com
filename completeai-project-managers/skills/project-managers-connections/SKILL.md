# Connections (MCP servers) for Project Managers

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit project managers
1. **Google Workspace MCP server**: Lets your AI read and write your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and more, all from one chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/taylorwilsdon-google-workspace-mcp/
2. **Teamwork MCP server** (official): Connect your AI to Teamwork so it can look up projects, tasks, tickets, and messages for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/teamwork-mcp/
3. **Excel MCP server**: Lets your AI create, read and edit Excel workbooks for you, without opening Excel.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/haris-musa-excel-mcp-server/
4. **WayStation MCP server** (no install needed): Connect your AI to Notion, Slack, Jira, Airtable and other work apps so it can look things up and update them for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/waystation-ai-mcp/
5. **Google Sheets MCP server**: Lets your AI read, write, and manage your Google Sheets for you, right from the chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/xing5-mcp-google-sheets/
6. **Box MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI search your Box files, answer questions about them, and pull out details for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/box-mcp-server-box-remote/
7. **Google Drive MCP server**: Lets your AI search and read your Google Drive files and read or edit your Google Sheets.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/isaacphi-mcp-gdrive/
8. **Procore MCP server**: Lets your AI look up and work with your Procore projects, RFIs, submittals, daily logs and budgets.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/tylerilunga-procore-mcp-server/
9. **ChatterBox MCP server**: Send a ChatterBox bot to your Zoom or Google Meet call and get the transcript, recording, and a summary.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/overquotaai-chatterboxio-mcp-server/
10. **Atlassian MCP server**: Lets your AI search, read, and update your Jira tickets and Confluence pages for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/sooperset-mcp-atlassian/
11. **Quip MCP server**: Lets your AI read, search, create, edit and delete your Quip documents and comments for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/bug-breeder-quip-mcp/
12. **Feishu MCP server**: Let your AI read, search and edit your Feishu documents for you, after you log in with your Feishu account.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ztxtxwd-open-feishu-mcp-server/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
