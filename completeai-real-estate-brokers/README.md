# Complete AI for Real Estate Brokers

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for real estate brokers. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **real-estate-brokers-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for real estate brokers
- **An app to build with AI**: Property listing written and video marketing workbench. Build this to cut listing marketing time while keeping your approved wording and branding on every channel.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like SignWell MCP server, OfficeAgent MCP server, Propstack MCP server

## Try it
- "Write a listing description for 412 Oak St"
- "Build a CMA for a 3-bed ranch in my zip"
- "Draft a follow-up email for a cold buyer"
- "Price this listing: 4 bed, 2 bath, 2,100 sq ft"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for real estate brokers ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for real estate brokers (351 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for real estate brokers on completeaitraining.com, like the Fair Housing Communication Review Agent and the Buyer Closing Readiness Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Real Estate Brokers" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add nexibeo/plugins-completeaitraining.com`, then `/plugin install completeai-real-estate-brokers@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
