# Build a Property listing written and video marketing workbench

Build this to cut listing marketing time while keeping your approved wording and branding on every channel.

- Who it is for: Real estate agents, brokers and property marketing teams producing listing content
- The problem it solves: Listing copy, social posts and video reels are produced in separate tools, so property marketing is slow and inconsistent.
- What goes in: Licensed property details, photos, video clips, brand assets and market notes
- What comes out: Agent-approved listing copy, social posts and video reels linked to each listing
- See it working (online demo): https://completeaitraining.com/build-ai-apps/real-estate-and-construction/property-listing-written-and-video-marketing-workbench/

How to help:
1. Run the two-minute version below: it builds a clickable first version right here in the chat.
2. Improve it with the user, one change at a time.
