# Build a Property listing written and video marketing workbench

Build this to cut listing marketing time while keeping your approved wording and branding on every channel.

- Who it is for: Real estate agents, brokers and property marketing teams producing listing content
- The problem it solves: Listing copy, social posts and video reels are produced in separate tools, so property marketing is slow and inconsistent.
- What goes in: Licensed property details, photos, video clips, brand assets and market notes
- What comes out: Agent-approved listing copy, social posts and video reels linked to each listing
- See it working (online demo): https://completeaitraining.com/build-ai-apps/real-estate-and-construction/property-listing-written-and-video-marketing-workbench/

How to help:
1. Start with the two-minute version: run its prompt and build the clickable first version here.
2. Want the real app? Follow "Build the real app" below, phase by phase.

## The two-minute version

```
Build me a simple working version of an app called "Property listing written and video marketing workbench" that I can click through right here in this chat (as an interactive page, artifact or canvas).

Who it is for: Real estate agents, brokers and property marketing teams producing listing content
The problem it solves: Listing copy, social posts and video reels are produced in separate tools, so property marketing is slow and inconsistent.
What I put in: Licensed property details, photos, video clips, brand assets and market notes
What I get out: Agent-approved listing copy, social posts and video reels linked to each listing

What it should do, step by step:
1. Capture property details and listing facts
2. Generate listing descriptions from details and images
3. Support luxury, vacation and commercial property types
4. Apply tone options such as confident, joyful or respectful
5. Suggest market-insight and buyer-persona wording
6. Produce social media posts per platform
7. Convert approved text into video with synchronized voiceover
8. Offer multiple AI voice narration options
9. Apply customizable video templates and brand styles
10. Add images, video clips and background music
11. Generate short video reels
12. Export videos in resolutions for social media and presentations
13. Compare the reviewed result with the recorded baseline and value assumptions
14. Capture corrections and named-owner approval before publishing
15. Export a versioned agent-approved listing package with source references and unresolved questions

Please:
- Put it all on one page and fill it with realistic sample data, so I can try it straight away.
- Make the buttons work, even if some results are simulated for now.
- I don't know how to code: don't ask me to install anything, and explain in plain language how to use it.
- When it's ready, suggest three improvements I could ask you for next.
```
