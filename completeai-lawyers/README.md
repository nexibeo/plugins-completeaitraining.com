# Complete AI for Lawyers

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for lawyers. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **lawyers-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for lawyers
- **An app to build with AI**: Evidence-backed legal research and document review workspace. Build this to cut research time while every finding links back to the source you cited.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like OfficeAgent MCP server, Box MCP server, Word document MCP server

## Try it
- "Draft an NDA for a vendor meeting next week"
- "Review this contract and flag the risky clauses"
- "Summarize these three cases on breach of duty"
- "Check my citations before I file the brief"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for lawyers ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for lawyers (343 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for lawyers on completeaitraining.com, like the Contract Obligation Calendar Agent and the Litigation Exhibit Assembly Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Lawyers" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add completeaitraining/completeai-plugins`, then `/plugin install completeai-lawyers@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
