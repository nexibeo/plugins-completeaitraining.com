# Connections (MCP servers) for Lawyers

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit lawyers
1. **OfficeAgent MCP server** (official): Let your AI edit Word, PowerPoint and Excel files, with changes you can review before saving.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ilia-sokolov-officeagent-net/
2. **Box MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI search your Box files, answer questions about them, and pull out details for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/box-mcp-server-box-remote/
3. **Word document MCP server**: Let your AI open, edit, and review Word files, with changes shown as tracked revisions.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/securityronin-docx-mcp/
4. **KitchenSink4Word MCP server**: Let your AI edit real Word documents: text, tables, tracked changes, citations and more.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/nometalalchemist-kitchensink4word/
5. **SignWell MCP server** (official): Send documents for e-signature, track who signed, and download the signed PDFs, all from your AI chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/bidsketch-signwell-mcp/
6. **Outlook Attachment Reader MCP server**: Let your AI open and read the files attached to your Outlook emails, and send local files as drafts.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/zacccck-claude-mcp-read-email-attachments/
7. **Web Researcher MCP server**: Lets your AI search trusted sites, read full articles, and give you real, clickable sources.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/zoharbabin-web-researcher-mcp/
8. **LibreJustice MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Search French and European case law and legal texts, and read any article as it stood on a given date.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/librejustice-librejustice/
9. **CanLII MCP server** (no install needed): Lets your AI look up Canadian court cases and laws by title, citation, date, and keywords.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/vaquill-ai-canlii-mcp/
10. **Normativa Colombia MCP server**: Consulta leyes y sentencias colombianas desde tu asistente de IA, sin abrir el navegador.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/angelthebestone-normativa-colombiana-mcp/
11. **Sudact MCP server**: Let your AI search Russian court decisions and pull up the full text of a case for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/atomno-mcp-mcp-sudact/
12. **LexAPI MCP server**: Look up EU laws, court cases, and citations from EUR-Lex right inside your AI chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/lex-api-lexapi-mcp/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
