# Complete AI for Sales representatives

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for sales representatives. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **sales-representatives-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for sales representatives
- **An app to build with AI**: Sales outreach sequence and follow-up workbench. Build sequences, personalize them, send, update CRM and triage replies in one portal you own.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like HeyLead LinkedIn outreach MCP server, Linked API MCP server, Pipedrive MCP server

## Try it
- "Write a follow-up email for yesterday's demo"
- "Qualify these 20 leads and rank them"
- "Help me handle the price objection"
- "Draft a cold email sequence for CFOs"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for sales representatives ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for sales representatives (300 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for sales representatives on completeaitraining.com, like the Sales Proposal Assembly Agent and the Sales Account Research Gap Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Sales representatives" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add completeaitraining/completeai-plugins`, then `/plugin install completeai-sales-representatives@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
