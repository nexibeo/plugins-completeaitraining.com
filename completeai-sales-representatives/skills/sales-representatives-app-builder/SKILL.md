# Build a Sales outreach sequence and follow-up workbench

Build sequences, personalize them, send, update CRM and triage replies in one portal you own.

- Who it is for: Sales and marketing teams running multi-step email outreach to their own prospect lists
- The problem it solves: Outreach and follow-ups are split across several rented tools, so sequences, personalization, CRM updates and reply handling drift apart and data sits outside the team's control.
- What goes in: Licensed contact lists, CRM records, brand and tone rules and sending-account settings
- What comes out: A reviewed outreach sequence with follow-ups and a prioritized reply queue
- See it working (online demo): https://completeaitraining.com/build-ai-apps/sales/sales-outreach-sequence-and-follow-up-workbench/

How to help:
1. Run the two-minute version below: it builds a clickable first version right here in the chat.
2. Improve it with the user, one change at a time.
