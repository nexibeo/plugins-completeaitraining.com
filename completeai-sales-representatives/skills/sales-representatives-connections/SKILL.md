# Connections (MCP servers) for Sales Representatives

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit sales representatives
1. **HeyLead LinkedIn outreach MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI find LinkedIn leads, write messages in your voice, follow up, and handle replies for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/d4umak-linkedin-outreach-mcp/
2. **Linked API MCP server** (official): Lets your AI search LinkedIn, read profiles and conversations, and send messages for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/linked-api-linkedapi-mcp/
3. **Pipedrive MCP server**: Let your AI look up, create, and update your Pipedrive deals, contacts, and activities for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ckalima-pipedrive-mcp-server/
4. **Apollo.io MCP server**: Your AI can search for sales prospects and look up contact and company details using Apollo.io.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/louis030195-apollo-io-mcp/
5. **B2B Enrichment MCP server**: Find and verify work emails, and pull company details like size and funding, right inside your AI chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/aleksey-panf-b2b-enrichment-mcp/
6. **ScraperCity MCP server**: Lets your AI pull business leads, find and check emails, and look up phone numbers for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/scrapercity-scrapercity-cli/
7. **Capsule CRM MCP server**: Ask your AI to find, create, and update contacts, deals, and tasks in your Capsule CRM account.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/monadsag-capsulecrm-mcp/
8. **Domain to LinkedIn URL Resolver MCP server**: Find the LinkedIn company page for a company name or website, with a confidence score and company details.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/mambalabsdev-mcp-domain-to-linkedin-url-resolver/
9. **Veezee MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Lets your AI look up LinkedIn, Reddit and X profiles, companies, posts and search results for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/veezeehq-veezee-mcp/
10. **DoubleTick email tracking server**: Send emails from your AI and see whether they were opened, plus when and on what device.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/cseguinlz-doubletick-cli/
11. **LeadAce MCP server**: Let your AI research prospects, draft one email each, send from your Gmail, and track replies.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/aitit-inc-leadace/
12. **Pearch.ai MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI search for people and companies in plain words and pull up their profiles.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/pearch-ai-mcp-pearch/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
