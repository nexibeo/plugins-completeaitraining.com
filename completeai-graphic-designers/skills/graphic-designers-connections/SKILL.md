# Connections (MCP servers) for Graphic Designers

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit graphic designers
1. **RNV Color MCP server** (no install needed): Mix, convert, and match colors, build palettes, and save them by name, right from your chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/rnvizion-rnv-color-mcp/
2. **Colorblind Safety MCP servers**: Check if your colors and images are colorblind-safe, and see them the way a colorblind person does.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/vince-gonzalez-opticquiz-mcp/
3. **AI Vision MCP server**: Lets your AI look at images and videos, describe them, compare them, and check design quality.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/tan-yong-sheng-ai-vision-mcp/
4. **StudioMCPHub MCP server** (no install needed): Lets your AI make and edit images, prep print files, and look up museum artworks for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/codex-curator-studiomcphub/
5. **CoDesign MCP server**: Your AI can create, edit and export branded designs like social posts, flyers and print PDFs.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/imgly-codesign/
6. **Unsplash MCP server**: Lets your AI search Unsplash photos, collections and topics, with ready-made photo credits.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/hanoak-unsplash-mcp-server/
7. **Pexels MCP server**: Your AI can search free Pexels stock photos and videos and bring back links and credits.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/afshinator-mcp-server-pexels/
8. **webfetch MCP server** (no install needed): Your AI can search for images across many sources and tell you the license and credit line for each one.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ashlrai-webfetch/
9. **Brand System MCP server**: Your AI can read your brand guidelines, make on-brand content, and check drafts against your rules.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/brand-system-brandsystem-mcp/
10. **Stocky stock photo MCP server**: Search and download free stock photos from Pexels and Unsplash right from your AI chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/joelio-stocky/
11. **Topaz Labs MCP server**: Let your AI improve your photos with Topaz Labs: upscale, sharpen, and clean up images for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/topazlabs-topaz-mcp/
12. **OpenAI GPT Image MCP server**: Lets your AI create and edit images with OpenAI's image models, right from the chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/surescaleai-openai-gpt-image-mcp/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
