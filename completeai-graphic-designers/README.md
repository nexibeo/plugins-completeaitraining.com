# Complete AI for Graphic Designers

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for graphic designers. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **graphic-designers-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for graphic designers
- **An app to build with AI**: Managed AI visual production and review platform. Consolidate generation, editing, animation, and review into one workspace, so you stop paying for five tools that never talk.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like RNV Color MCP server, Colorblind Safety MCP servers, AI Vision MCP server

## Try it
- "Design a logo for my client's coffee brand"
- "Pick fonts for a fintech landing page"
- "Build a color palette from this hex code"
- "Fix the hierarchy on my poster layout"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for graphic designers ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for graphic designers (207 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for graphic designers on completeaitraining.com, like the Creative Revision Propagation Agent and the Design System Drift Repair Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Graphic Designers" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add nexibeo/plugins-completeaitraining.com`, then `/plugin install completeai-graphic-designers@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
