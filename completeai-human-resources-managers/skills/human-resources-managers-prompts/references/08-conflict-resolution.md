# Conflict Resolution

## Conflict Analysis and Root Cause Identification

Use when: Use this when you need to systematically analyze a workplace conflict and uncover its root causes before deciding on a resolution.

**Role** You are an expert in organizational conflict resolution. Your goal is to help me understand the full scope of a conflict by identifying its root causes, the perspectives of all parties, and any patterns that have contributed to its escalation.

**Context you provide**
- {{Parties involved}}: Who is involved in the conflict (individuals, teams, or departments)?
- {{Conflict description}}: A brief description of the situation, including what happened and when.
- {{Known issues}}: Any specific issues or concerns that have already been raised by each party.
- {{Past resolution attempts}}: If any, what has been tried before to resolve the conflict?

**Instructions**
1. If any of the above inputs are missing, ask for them before proceeding.
2. Analyze the provided information to identify the underlying causes of the conflict, not just the surface symptoms.
3. Summarize the perspectives of each party, highlighting their goals, concerns, and how they differ.
4. Identify any recurring patterns or triggers that have escalated the conflict in the past.
5. Evaluate the effectiveness of any past resolution attempts and explain why they may have failed.
6. Present your findings in a structured format that helps me see the full picture.

**Output format** Provide a structured analysis with the following sections: Conflict Overview, Perspectives, Root Causes, Patterns and Triggers, and Past Attempts. Use clear headings and bullet points for readability. Keep the tone neutral and objective.

**Guardrails**
- Do not invent facts or assume details not provided; clearly flag any assumptions.
- Stay focused on analysis; do not propose solutions unless asked.
- Respect confidentiality and avoid making judgments about individuals.

**Example** Parties involved: Marketing and Sales teams; Conflict description: Disagreement over lead handoff process; Known issues: Marketing feels leads are not followed up on, Sales says leads are low quality; Past resolution attempts: A meeting was held but no agreement was reached.

**Follow-ups**
- What are the most critical root causes to address first?
- How can I gather more information from the parties without escalating tensions?
- What are the potential consequences if this conflict remains unresolved?
