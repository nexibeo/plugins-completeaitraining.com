# Connections (MCP servers) for Teachers

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit teachers
1. **Keynote MCP server**: Let your AI make and edit Apple Keynote slideshows on your Mac just by asking.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/byaxe-keynote-mcp/
2. **Google Workspace MCP server**: Lets your AI read and write your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and more, all from one chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/taylorwilsdon-google-workspace-mcp/
3. **Presentations.AI MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI build and edit slide decks for you, then export them as PPTX, PDF, images or a share link.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/slidecraft-in-presentations-ai-mcp-server/
4. **ICTExam MCP server**: Let your AI read your ICTExam papers, gradebook and question analysis, and publish exams when you allow it.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ictinnovations-ictexam-mcp/
5. **Moodle MCP server**: Ask your AI about your Moodle site and get back courses, users, assignments and site info.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/csmediapro-moodle-mcp-server/
6. **Google Drive MCP server**: Lets your AI search and read your Google Drive files and read or edit your Google Sheets.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/isaacphi-mcp-gdrive/
7. **Wolfram Alpha MCP server**: Lets your AI look up facts, do math, and answer science and data questions through Wolfram Alpha.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/ricocf-mcp-wolframalpha/
8. **VE Google Workspace MCP server**: Let your AI read and write your Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Gmail and Calendar for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/huntsdesk-ve-gws/
9. **Google MCP server**: Lets your AI read and send Gmail, manage Calendar events, browse Drive files and handle Tasks for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/vakharwalad23-google-mcp/
10. **YouTube MCP server**: Lets your AI pull YouTube video transcripts, details, search results, channel info and playlists when you ask.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/klavis-ai-klavis-youtube/
11. **Media Gen MCP server**: Create and edit images and videos with OpenAI and Google tools, right from your AI chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/strato-space-media-gen-mcp/
12. **Outlook Assistant MCP server**: Lets your AI read, search, send, and organise your Outlook email, calendar, and contacts from the chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/littlebearapps-outlook-assistant/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
