# Complete AI for Teachers

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for teachers. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **teachers-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for teachers
- **An app to build with AI**: Assessment feedback assistant. Build feedback from your own rubric so students get clear next steps and you keep every score.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like Keynote MCP server, Google Workspace MCP server, Presentations.AI MCP server

## Try it
- "Lesson plan for 8th grade Civil War, 50 minutes"
- "Write 10 quiz questions on fractions for grade 5"
- "Give feedback on this student essay draft"
- "My class won't settle after lunch. What works?"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for teachers ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for teachers (195 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for teachers on completeaitraining.com, like the Lab Teaching Worksheet Agent and the Missing Work Recovery Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Teachers" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add nexibeo/plugins-completeaitraining.com`, then `/plugin install completeai-teachers@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
