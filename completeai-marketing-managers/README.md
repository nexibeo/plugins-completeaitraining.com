# Complete AI for Marketing managers

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for marketing managers. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **18 skills**, starting with **marketing-managers-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for marketing managers
- **An app to build with AI**: Multi-channel campaign copy production workspace. Build this to produce campaign copy for every channel from one approved brand voice, cutting review cycles.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like PostMCP social posting server, Meta Ads MCP server, Mailchimp MCP server

## Try it
- "Draft a launch email for our new pricing page"
- "Write 5 LinkedIn posts about our webinar"
- "Compare us to our top 3 competitors"
- "Plan a 4-week email drip for new leads"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for marketing managers ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for marketing managers (284 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for marketing managers on completeaitraining.com, like the Marketing Asset Claim Alignment Agent and the Webinar Follow-up Segmentation Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Marketing managers" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add completeaitraining/completeai-plugins`, then `/plugin install completeai-marketing-managers@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
