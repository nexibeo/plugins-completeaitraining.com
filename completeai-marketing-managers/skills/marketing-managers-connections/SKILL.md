# Connections (MCP servers) for Marketing Managers

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit marketing managers
1. **PostMCP social posting server** (official): Lets your AI draft, check, schedule and publish posts to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky and YouTube Shorts.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/postmcp-postmcp-mcp-server/
2. **Meta Ads MCP server**: Let your AI check, create and adjust your Facebook and Instagram ads for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/mikusnuz-meta-ads-mcp/
3. **Mailchimp MCP server**: Lets your AI look up and manage your Mailchimp campaigns, audiences and reports for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/damientilman-mailchimp-mcp-server/
4. **Facebook Ads MCP server**: Lets your AI look up your Facebook ad accounts, campaigns, ads and their performance numbers for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/gomarble-ai-facebook-ads-mcp-server/
5. **Facebook Page MCP server**: Let your AI write, schedule and moderate Facebook Page posts, and pull engagement numbers for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/hagaihen-facebook-mcp-server/
6. **AdsAgent MCP server** (no install needed): Lets your AI read and prepare changes for your Meta, Google Ads, and TikTok ad accounts.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/adsagents-adsagent-ai-skills/
7. **TikTok Ads MCP server**: Ask your AI about your TikTok ad campaigns, video performance, and benchmarks in plain words.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/dhawalshah-tiktok-ads-mcp/
8. **Synter Ads MCP server** (no install needed): Let your AI check, create, and adjust ad campaigns across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and more.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/synter-media-ai-mcp-server/
9. **Reddit Ads MCP server**: Lets your AI read your Reddit ad campaigns and reports, and make changes like pausing or budget edits when you allow it.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/camlowe-mcp-server-reddit-ads/
10. **SEOcrawl AI MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Ask your AI for live Google Search Console, GA4 and AI visibility data, plus site audits and SEO tasks.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/seocrawl-seocrawl-mcp/
11. **Google Ads MCP server**: Lets your AI read your Google Ads accounts, run reports, and find keyword ideas for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/gomarble-ai-google-ads-mcp-server/
12. **mureo MCP server**: Let your AI check and improve your Google Ads, Meta Ads and Search Console accounts using your own strategy notes.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/logly-mureo/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
