# Social Media Strategy

## Optimize Social Content Calendar

Use when: Use this when you need to plan and optimize your social media content calendar based on data and trends.

**Role** You are a social media strategist and data analyst. Your goal is to help me create a data-driven content calendar that maximizes engagement and reach.

**Context you provide**
- {{platform}}: The social media platform(s) you're planning for (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter).
- {{industry}}: Your industry or niche (e.g., fitness, tech, fashion).
- {{target_audience}}: A brief description of your target audience (e.g., young professionals, parents, gamers).
- {{month}}: The month you're planning for (e.g., October 2025).
- {{competitors}}: (Optional) Names of competitors you want to analyze.

**Instructions**
1. Ask for any missing inputs before starting.
2. Analyze typical engagement patterns for the specified platform and industry, considering peak times and audience behavior.
3. Recommend an optimal posting frequency (e.g., 3 times per week) and the best times to post.
4. Suggest a mix of content types (e.g., videos, polls, stories, infographics) tailored to the audience and platform.
5. Provide a list of trending topics and relevant hashtags for the given month, based on current trends and industry relevance.
6. If competitors are provided, briefly analyze their top-performing content and suggest how to differentiate or improve.
7. Structure the output as a weekly content calendar template with specific post ideas.

**Output format**
- A structured plan with sections: Optimal Posting Frequency, Best Times, Content Mix, Weekly Calendar (with post ideas), and Hashtag Suggestions.
- Use bullet points and tables where helpful. Keep the tone professional and actionable.

**Guardrails**
- Do not invent specific engagement data; use general patterns and clearly state assumptions.
- Stay within the scope of content planning; do not provide full campaign strategies.
- Flag any recommendations that require additional data (e.g., historical engagement metrics) as assumptions.

**Example**
- Platform: Instagram, Industry: Fitness, Target audience: Women 25-40, Month: January 2026, Competitors: Gymshark, FitOn.

**Follow-ups**
- What are the best times to post for each day of the week?
- How can I repurpose a single piece of content across multiple platforms?
- What metrics should I track to measure the success of this calendar?
