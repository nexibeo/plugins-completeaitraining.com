# Build a Multi-channel campaign copy production workspace

Build this to produce campaign copy for every channel from one approved brand voice, cutting review cycles.

- Who it is for: Marketing teams and agencies producing campaign copy across several channels
- The problem it solves: Campaign copy is generated in several rented tools, so brand voice, audience rules and approvals are scattered and hard to reuse.
- What goes in: Approved brand rules, audience segments, product facts and channel specifications
- What comes out: Editor-approved campaign copy linked to source claims
- See it working (online demo): https://completeaitraining.com/build-ai-apps/marketing/multi-channel-campaign-copy-production-workspace/

How to help:
1. Start with the two-minute version: run its prompt and build the clickable first version here.
2. Want the real app? Follow "Build the real app" below, phase by phase.

## The two-minute version

```
Build me a simple working version of an app called "Multi-channel campaign copy production workspace" that I can click through right here in this chat (as an interactive page, artifact or canvas).

Who it is for: Marketing teams and agencies producing campaign copy across several channels
The problem it solves: Campaign copy is generated in several rented tools, so brand voice, audience rules and approvals are scattered and hard to reuse.
What I put in: Approved brand rules, audience segments, product facts and channel specifications
What I get out: Editor-approved campaign copy linked to source claims

What it should do, step by step:
1. Generate channel-specific copy from approved inputs
2. Apply templates for emails, ads, landing pages and social posts
3. Edit, rephrase and regenerate selected passages
4. Score copy against stated performance criteria
5. Tailor copy to defined audience segments and personas
6. Flag words that may trigger spam filters
7. Repurpose existing content into other formats
8. Generate and rank subject line variants
9. Apply approved design and layout options
10. Assemble funnel steps with pre-filled scripts
11. Sync approved copy to connected platforms
12. Keep version history for comparison
13. Build a page draft from a product link
14. Store generation history for reuse
15. Compare the reviewed result with the recorded baseline and value assumptions
16. Capture corrections and named-owner approval before consequential use
17. Export a versioned editor-approved campaign copy linked to source claims with source references and unresolved questions

Please:
- Put it all on one page and fill it with realistic sample data, so I can try it straight away.
- Make the buttons work, even if some results are simulated for now.
- I don't know how to code: don't ask me to install anything, and explain in plain language how to use it.
- When it's ready, suggest three improvements I could ask you for next.
```
