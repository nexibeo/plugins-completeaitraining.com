# Connections (MCP servers) for Administrative Assistants

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit administrative assistants
1. **Google Workspace MCP server**: Lets your AI read and send Gmail, manage Calendar events, and work with Drive files for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/giuseppe-coco-google-workspace-mcp-server/
2. **Microsoft 365 MCP server**: Lets your AI read and send Outlook mail, check your calendar, and work with OneDrive and Excel files.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/softeria-ms-365-mcp-server/
3. **Outlook Assistant MCP server**: Lets your AI read, search, send, and organise your Outlook email, calendar, and contacts from the chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/littlebearapps-outlook-assistant/
4. **Temporal Cortex MCP server** (no install needed): Let your AI check calendars, find free slots, and book meetings without double-booking.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/temporal-cortex-mcp/
5. **Google MCP server**: Lets your AI read and send Gmail, manage Calendar events, browse Drive files and handle Tasks for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/vakharwalad23-google-mcp/
6. **GigaMail MCP server**: Let your AI read, search and draft your email, with you approving every send or delete.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/adecubed-gigamail/
7. **Google Calendar MCP server**: Lets your AI read, create, change, and delete events in your Google Calendar for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/takumi0706-google-calendar-mcp/
8. **Meet.bot MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI check your Meet.bot calendar availability and book meetings for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/poolside-ventures-meetbot-mcp/
9. **MCP Emails server** (no install needed): Let your AI read, search, send and organize email in your own mailbox.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/albretsen-mcpemails/
10. **Microsoft Graph MCP server**: Lets your AI read your Outlook mail and calendar, Teams chats, files and notes inside Microsoft 365.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/nitin27may-ms-graph-mcp/
11. **Outlook Local MCP server**: Lets your AI read your Outlook calendar and mail, and write draft emails for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/desek-outlook-local-mcp/
12. **Gmail and Google Calendar MCP server**: Let your AI read your Gmail, write drafts, and check or create Google Calendar events for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/markuspfundstein-mcp-gsuite/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
