# Complete AI for Administrative Assistants

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for administrative assistants. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **administrative-assistants-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for administrative assistants
- **An app to build with AI**: Email-to-task coordination portal. Use it when inbox requests become tasks, so every email has an owner, a due date and a review step.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like Google Workspace MCP server, Microsoft 365 MCP server, Outlook Assistant MCP server

## Try it
- "Draft a reminder email about overdue expense reports."
- "Turn these meeting notes into actions and owners."
- "Build an agenda for our monthly HR all-hands."
- "Plan a two-day client trip to Chicago, options only."

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for administrative assistants ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for administrative assistants (328 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for administrative assistants on completeaitraining.com, like the Interview Logistics Recovery Agent and the Office Event and RSVP Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Administrative Assistants" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add completeaitraining/completeai-plugins`, then `/plugin install completeai-administrative-assistants@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
