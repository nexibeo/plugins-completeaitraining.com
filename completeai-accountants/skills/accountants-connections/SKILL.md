# Connections (MCP servers) for Accountants

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. Each connection gets access to that app, so you add only the ones you choose.

## The connections that fit accountants
1. **Xero MCP server**: Lets your AI look up and create Xero invoices, contacts, payments and reports for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/xeroapi-xero-mcp-server/
2. **Box MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI search your Box files, answer questions about them, and pull out details for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/box-mcp-server-box-remote/
3. **Synder GL Importer for QuickBooks and Xero** (official): Let your AI import CSV or Excel accounting files into QuickBooks Online or Xero for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/synderaccounting-gl-importer-plugin/
4. **Excel MCP server**: Let your AI assistant open and work inside real Microsoft Excel on your Windows PC.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/sbroenne-mcp-server-excel/
5. **Odoo 19 MCP server**: Let your AI look up, add, and update records in your Odoo 19 company system for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/twtrubiks-odoo19-mcp-server/
6. **Google Sheets MCP server**: Lets your AI read, write, and format your Google Sheets for you, right from the chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/freema-mcp-gsheets/
7. **Outlook Attachment Reader MCP server**: Let your AI open and read the files attached to your Outlook emails, and send local files as drafts.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/zacccck-claude-mcp-read-email-attachments/
8. **1C:Enterprise MCP server**: Lets your AI read and update data in your 1C:Enterprise ERP system, all from a chat.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/infaton-mcp35/
9. **Odoo MCP server**: Let your AI read and safely update your Odoo business data, with your approval before any change.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/tuanle96-mcp-odoo/
10. **camt053 MCP server**: Let your AI read ISO 20022 bank statement files, check them, and build reversing entries for you.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/sebastienrousseau-camt053-mcp/
11. **Czech National Bank FX rates MCP server** (no install needed): Your AI can look up official Czech National Bank exchange rates and convert amounts to or from Czech koruna.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/martinhavel-cz-agents-mcp/
12. **Spreadsheet MCP server**: Lets your AI read, edit, and recalculate Excel files safely, without you opening Excel.
   About it, and how to add it: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/psu3d0-spreadsheet-mcp/

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "How do I connect <app>?": give the link to that connection's page. The user follows the steps on that page themselves. Do not install or set up anything, and never ask for passwords or keys in the chat.
