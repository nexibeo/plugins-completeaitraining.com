# Connections (MCP servers) for Accountants

A connection (an MCP server) lets your AI look things up and get work done in an app you already use, like your email, files or bookkeeping software. You only add the ones for the apps you use: each connection gets access to that app, so it's always your choice, one app at a time.

## The connections that fit Accountants
1. **Xero MCP server**: Lets your AI look up and create Xero invoices, contacts, payments and reports for you.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/xeroapi-xero-mcp-server/
2. **Box MCP server** (official) (no install needed): Let your AI search your Box files, answer questions about them, and pull out details for you.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/box-mcp-server-box-remote/
3. **Synder GL Importer for QuickBooks and Xero** (official): Let your AI import CSV or Excel accounting files into QuickBooks Online or Xero for you.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/synderaccounting-gl-importer-plugin/
4. **Excel MCP server**: Let your AI assistant open and work inside real Microsoft Excel on your Windows PC.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/sbroenne-mcp-server-excel/
5. **Odoo 19 MCP server**: Let your AI look up, add, and update records in your Odoo 19 company system for you.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/twtrubiks-odoo19-mcp-server/
6. **Google Sheets MCP server**: Lets your AI read, write, and format your Google Sheets for you, right from the chat.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/freema-mcp-gsheets/
7. **Outlook Attachment Reader MCP server**: Let your AI open and read the files attached to your Outlook emails, and send local files as drafts.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/zacccck-claude-mcp-read-email-attachments/
8. **1C:Enterprise MCP server**: Lets your AI read and update data in your 1C:Enterprise ERP system, all from a chat.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/infaton-mcp35/
9. **Odoo MCP server**: Let your AI read and safely update your Odoo business data, with your approval before any change.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/tuanle96-mcp-odoo/
10. **camt053 MCP server**: Let your AI read ISO 20022 bank statement files, check them, and build reversing entries for you.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/sebastienrousseau-camt053-mcp/
11. **Czech National Bank FX rates MCP server** (no install needed): Your AI can look up official Czech National Bank exchange rates and convert amounts to or from Czech koruna.
   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/martinhavel-cz-agents-mcp/

   Set-up steps and a copy-and-paste install prompt: https://completeaitraining.com/mcp-servers/psu3d0-spreadsheet-mcp/

## How to add one (we guide you)
1. Open the connection's page (the link above) and pick the AI you use.
2. Follow the steps there, or copy the install prompt and paste it into Claude: it checks the connection and installs it with you, step by step.
3. In ChatGPT: Settings → Apps & Connectors → Add, then paste the connection's address from its page.
4. In Microsoft 365 Copilot at work: ask your IT team to add it (the page has a section for them).

## When the user asks
- "Which apps can you connect to?": list the connections above in plain words and ask which apps they use.
- "Help me connect <app>": open the matching connection's page link and walk them through its steps one at a time. Never ask for passwords or keys in the chat: the app's own sign-in screen handles that.
