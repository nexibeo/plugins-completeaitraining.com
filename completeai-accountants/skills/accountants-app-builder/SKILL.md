# Build a Receipt capture and expense data stewardship console

Build this to cut manual receipt entry while keeping a reviewable record of every extracted field for audit.

- Who it is for: Small finance teams, bookkeepers and accountants managing client or company expense records
- The problem it solves: Receipts arrive as paper, email and bank notices, and staff retype merchant, date, amount and tax into spreadsheets and accounting systems.
- What goes in: Captured receipts, forwarded e-receipts, bank emails, accounting categories and tax rules
- What comes out: Reviewer-approved expense records linked to source documents
- See it working (online demo): https://completeaitraining.com/build-ai-apps/finance/receipt-capture-and-expense-data-stewardship-console/

How to help:
1. Start with the two-minute version: run its prompt and build the clickable first version here.
2. Want the real app? Follow "Build the real app" below, phase by phase.

## The two-minute version

```
Build me a simple working version of an app called "Receipt capture and expense data stewardship console" that I can click through right here in this chat (as an interactive page, artifact or canvas).

Who it is for: Small finance teams, bookkeepers and accountants managing client or company expense records
The problem it solves: Receipts arrive as paper, email and bank notices, and staff retype merchant, date, amount and tax into spreadsheets and accounting systems.
What I put in: Captured receipts, forwarded e-receipts, bank emails, accounting categories and tax rules
What I get out: Reviewer-approved expense records linked to source documents

What it should do, step by step:
1. Capture receipts by camera or mobile upload
2. Extract merchant, date, amount and tax with OCR and AI
3. Categorize expenses and income into accounting categories
4. Ingest forwarded e-receipts from a dedicated address
5. Capture expenses from bank notification emails
6. Store digitized receipts and documents in cloud storage
7. Handle expenses in multiple currencies
8. Sync approved records to accounting software
9. Export data to Excel, CSV and PDF
10. Let team members and accountants work on shared records
11. Give accountants view-only access
12. Calculate applicable taxes on expenses
13. Record expenses by voice command
14. Apply user-defined rules for AI categorization
15. Support documents and interface in multiple languages
16. Summarize long documents with adjustable length and highlighted key sentences
17. Compare the reviewed result with the recorded baseline and value assumptions
18. Capture corrections and named-owner approval before consequential use
19. Export a versioned reviewer-approved expense record linked to source documents with source references and unresolved questions

Please:
- Put it all on one page and fill it with realistic sample data, so I can try it straight away.
- Make the buttons work, even if some results are simulated for now.
- I don't know how to code: don't ask me to install anything, and explain in plain language how to use it.
- When it's ready, suggest three improvements I could ask you for next.
```
