# Complete AI for Accountants

A free plugin by Complete AI Training (https://completeaitraining.com) that turns Claude into a work assistant for accountants. Ask for the work in your own words and it picks the right skill, asks for the details it needs, and gives you the finished result.

## What's inside
- **19 skills**, starting with **accountants-assistant**, which picks the right one for each request
- **15 ready-made prompts** for accountants
- **An app to build with AI**: Receipt capture and expense data stewardship console. Build this to cut manual receipt entry while keeping a reviewable record of every extracted field for audit.
- **A connections guide**: the 12 apps your AI can connect to for this job (MCP servers), like Xero MCP server, Box MCP server, Synder GL Importer for QuickBooks and Xero

## Try it
- "Reconcile these bank statements for March"
- "Why is our actual spend over budget this quarter?"
- "Draft a cash flow forecast for the next 6 months"
- "Turn this receipt pile into a categorized expense list"

## For Complete AI Training members
The plugin includes the Complete AI Training connection (https://completeaitraining.com/mcp). Members sign in once with their account, and the plugin then adds:
- the daily AI news and a new idea every day for accountants ("Good morning")
- the full prompt library for accountants (290 prompts) and app build packs
- the weekly AI trends for the field

Members also get the 12 AI agents for accountants on completeaitraining.com, like the Cash Application Exception Agent and the Revenue Recognition Evidence Agent: https://completeaitraining.com/ai-agents/

Without an account, everything else in the plugin works as normal. About Complete AI Training: https://completeaitraining.com/pricing/

## What this plugin does with your data
- The skills and prompts run inside Claude. The plugin itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
- The Complete AI Training connection is used only when you sign in to it. It then reads your job profile and saves your learning progress, ideas you keep and your weekly review (categories and counts only). Privacy policy: https://completeaitraining.com/privacy-disclaimer/
- The connections guide links to other apps' pages. It never installs anything by itself.

## Install
- **claude.ai, the desktop app and Cowork:** find "Complete AI for Accountants" in the plugin directory and click Install. Then start a chat and type **/start**.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add nexibeo/plugins-completeaitraining.com`, then `/plugin install completeai-accountants@completeai-plugins`.

## Commands
- /start: what the plugin can do for you
- /prompts [task]: find a ready-made prompt
